# What are the aims?

I'm writing this to set the method and the strategy of my learning in the field of Software Systems and development.

## Learning Steps

On every topic you want to learn, master, apply the following questions and write the answers in a note combining all the info about that topic:

1. **What is `<Topic>`** → Sets a clear definition:
    
    - What is it
    - What it helps in
    - What it do
    - What it offers
2. **`<Topic>` Properties** → Sets a set of the featured things about the `<Topic>`. Note that this is the design properties.
    
3. **`<Topic>` Assumptions & Expectations** → Sets for you a clear vision of the outcomes and benefits of the given `<Topic>`.
    
4. **How `<Topic>` Works / Operate** → Sets the steps/workflow the given `<Topic>` do to meet its goals and how to do it rightly & implement the `<Topic>`.
    
5. **`<Topic>` Use Cases** → Sets down examples of systems / uses `<Topic>` and a general use cases in general situations. It is the "When" of the `<Topic>`.
    
6. **Pros & Cons** → Sets the downside/upside of `<Topic>` to enable you to judge it officially.
    
7. **Why We Needed `<Topic>`** → Shows the main problem and relates you to its history and decisions led to it.
    

## Learning Rules

- You cannot skip any step.
- You must search the web on each section and place the references at the end of the file.
